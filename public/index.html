  • New-arrival carousels: 4.30% ER. Carousels overall (3.14%) nearly match reels (3.37%)
  • #ootd, #officewear and #ethnicwear posts average 4.6–4.8% ER
  • Reels get strong distribution: on average they reach views equal to 68% of the follower count
  • Momentum is up: ER over the last 30 days is 8.1%, compared with 2.2% in the 30 days before

What doesn't

  • Greeting posts like "Happy Independence Day": 0.89% ER, about a third of the average
  • Hard-sell sale images: 1.52% ER
  • Single images overall: 1.13% ER, under half of reels and carousels
  • Posting at 9 AM and 8 PM, which both come in under 1.8% ER
  • Behind-the-scenes reels that show the craft without a hook or a payoff

Hooks

The best-performing openers promise something useful right away ("3 ways to style…") or start a familiar situation ("POV: …"). Behind-the-scenes reels open on the process with no stated payoff, which is probably why they underperform even though the craft story is strong. Sale and greeting posts have no hook at all.

Posting behaviour

Posts mostly go out at noon IST. The data shows 6–7 PM performs best (4.0% ER at 18:00, 3.7% at 19:00), and 9 AM performs worst (1.6%). Saturday is the best day (3.58%) and Tuesday the weakest (2.04%). Cadence is uneven: the average gap is 4.4 days, the longest is 12.4 days, and the consistency score is 57/100.

Gaps & opportunities

  • No series format. A recurring "Office look of the week" built on the top pillar would make posting regular
  • The craft story (Jaipur block print) is under-used. Add a hook and an outcome so it performs
  • Customer posts are under-used: turn UGC into try-on reels instead of repost carousels
  • Comments are low (2.9 per 100 likes). Captions rarely ask a question or prompt a reply
  • No creator collaborations or sponsored posts in the period analysed

Recommendations

1. Make styling reels the backbone

This is the best pillar at 4.78% ER, yet only 17% of posts.

Post 2 styling reels a week (office, wedding guest, college) with the payoff in the first 2 seconds.

2. Move posting to 6–7 PM, and post on Saturday

18:00 gets 4.0% ER versus 3.3% at the current usual time of noon.

Schedule the main reel at 6:30 PM IST. Keep Saturday for the strongest piece of the week.

3. Drop plain greeting and sale images

They score 0.9–1.5% ER and teach the algorithm that the audience skips the account.

Turn sales into a "3 looks under ₹1,500" reel and festivals into a styled-look carousel.

4. Fix the behind-the-scenes hook

The craft content is the brand's moat but sits at 2.2% ER.

Open with the finished kurta, then show "how 1 kurta takes 4 days". Add a counter or reveal at the end.

5. Post at a steady rhythm

12-day gaps break momentum. Consistency is 57/100.

Fix a 4-posts-a-week rhythm: 2 reels, 1 carousel, 1 UGC or try-on post.

6. Ask for comments

The account gets only 2.9 comments per 100 likes.

End captions with an either/or question ("Office or brunch?"), and pin a reply.
